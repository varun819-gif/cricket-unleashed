Connect to the game

  1. Put this phone on the same Wi-Fi as the TV or PC running Cricket Unleashed.
  2. On the TV, open Controllers. A QR code appears.
  3. Open the phone camera (or Google Lens) and point it at the QR code.
  4. Tap the link that appears. This page opens and connects by itself.

Each QR code works once and for 2 minutes. If it expires, the TV shows a new one.

Every ball is played out by the ball physics (speed, swing, bounce, spin, bat impact, fielders). No 3D graphics yet.
